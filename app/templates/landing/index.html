What is {{ site_name }}?

A home for technical knowledge

{{ site_name }} (short for MyDocument) lets anyone create an account and publish documents — articles, guides, notes and tutorials. Readers browse everything publicly; writers keep full control of their drafts with a Markdown editor, live preview and instant publishing.

{{ ui.icon('file-pen') }}

Write beautifully

A split-pane Markdown editor with toolbar, live preview, syntax-highlighted code blocks and autosaving drafts.

{{ ui.icon('search') }}

Discover instantly

Your feed learns from your interests and surfaces the latest published documents, with fast debounced search.

{{ ui.icon('shield') }}

Safe by design

Every document is sanitized server-side, CSRF-protected and moderated with a transparent report pipeline.

How it works

Three steps to your first document

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up for free with a username, e-mail and a strong password, then pick the topics you care about.

  2. 2

    Write in Markdown

    Open the split-view editor, add headings, code and images, watch the live preview, and autosave as you type.

  3. 3

    Publish & share

    Publish instantly to get a clean public URL, then track views, saves and reader feedback from your dashboard.
